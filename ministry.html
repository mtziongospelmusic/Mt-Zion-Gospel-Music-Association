Our Ministry

Serving God and communities through gospel music, worship, fellowship, outreach and Christian ministry.

Ministry Through Gospel Music

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to use the gift of gospel music as a means of sharing the message of faith, hope, love and salvation.

Through music and ministry activities, we seek to encourage believers, reach communities and create opportunities for gospel artists and musicians to serve together.

Our ministry is built around faith in God, unity, service and the responsible use of music to communicate positive Christian values.

Our Ministry Focus

To proclaim the Good News through gospel music while encouraging worship, fellowship, Christian service and community outreach.

Building a Culture of Worship

Gospel music can bring people together, encourage faith and create an atmosphere where people can reflect on God's Word.

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association aims to encourage musicians and worshippers to use their gifts with humility, excellence and purpose.

  • Encourage worship through gospel music
  • Support gospel musicians and singers
  • Promote positive Christian messages
  • Encourage unity among ministry workers
  • Create opportunities for gospel outreach

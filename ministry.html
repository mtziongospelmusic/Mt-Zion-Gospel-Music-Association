Our Ministry

Serving God, Encouraging People and Strengthening Communities

Faith • Music • Fellowship • Peace • Service

Ministry With Purpose

Our ministry seeks to connect gospel music and Christian faith with positive personal, family and community development.

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association believes that gospel music is a powerful way to proclaim the Good News of Jesus Christ, encourage faith and bring people together.

Our ministry vision extends beyond music. We seek to encourage children and young people, strengthen families, promote peace and reconciliation, support people who are marginalized and contribute positively to community development.

As the association develops, we intend to build ministry activities according to community needs, available resources and appropriate partnerships.

Our Ministry Areas

These areas represent the ministry direction Mt Zion seeks to develop and strengthen.

🎵 Gospel Music Ministry

Using gospel music, songs and musical creativity to proclaim the Good News, encourage people in faith and provide opportunities for musical gifts and talents to grow.

🙏 Worship & Fellowship

Encouraging worship, prayer, Christian fellowship and relationships that strengthen faith, hope, love and unity among individuals, families and communities.

👧 Children & Youth Ministry

Encouraging children and young people from childhood through adolescence to develop positive values, faith, confidence, respect, talents and a sense of responsibility.

🕊️ Peace & Reconciliation

Using Christian values, music, dialogue and community engagement to encourage forgiveness, respect, reconciliation, peaceful relationships and unity.

🤝 Community Service

Seeking opportunities to serve communities, support families and contribute to initiatives that improve community wellbeing.

❤️ Care & Inclusion

Giving attention to people who are marginalized, vulnerable or facing difficult circumstances, while encouraging dignity, inclusion, hope and participation.

Ministry & Community Development

We believe ministry can encourage both spiritual growth and positive community development.

🌱 Youth Development

Encourage young people to discover their gifts, develop useful skills and pursue constructive opportunities.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Strengthening

Encourage respect, responsibility, positive relationships and values that contribute to healthy families.

🕊️ Peaceful Communities

Promote peace, reconciliation, forgiveness, dialogue and unity through positive community engagement.

💧 Practical Community Needs

Seek ways to contribute to practical development priorities, including areas such as safe water and community wellbeing.

🤲 Supporting the Vulnerable

Seek appropriate ways to encourage and support marginalized and vulnerable people and families.

🎶 Music & Creativity

Use music and creativity as positive pathways for expression, encouragement, fellowship and youth participation.

Our Ministry Principles

We seek to build our ministry around faith, respect, service and responsible community engagement.

Faith

Jesus Christ and the Good News remain at the heart of our ministry.

Respect

We encourage people to respect one another regardless of background or circumstance.

Service

We seek opportunities to serve people and contribute positively to families and communities.

Peace

We encourage peaceful relationships, reconciliation and unity.

Hope

We want children, young people and families to see positive possibilities for their future.

Responsibility

We encourage people to use their gifts and abilities to make positive contributions to their families and communities.

Growing Our Ministry

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is developing a ministry that brings together gospel music, faith, youth development, peace, reconciliation and community service. As opportunities and resources become available, we seek to develop practical initiatives that serve people and strengthen communities.

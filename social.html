A Transparent Beginning

We believe supporters should understand where Mt Zion is in its development journey.

Current status: Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is currently working toward formal registration.

The association does not yet have an official organizational bank account. An official account is intended to be established after the registration process is completed.

Until formal arrangements are established, supporters are encouraged to contact the association directly through the official email address for information about how they may support the registration process.

We are committed to building the association on principles of honesty, accountability, responsible stewardship and service.

Why This Mission Matters

Mt Zion seeks to connect gospel music with positive community development and opportunities for people of all ages.

Gospel Music

Using music to proclaim the Good News, encourage faith, inspire hope and develop musical gifts.

Youth Development

Encouraging children and young people to develop their talents, character, confidence and sense of responsibility.

Peace & Reconciliation

Promoting respect, forgiveness, reconciliation and unity through gospel music, Christian values and community engagement.

Community Wellbeing

Seeking opportunities to support families, marginalized people and practical community needs as resources and partnerships grow.

Our Music Focus

Music is central to the vision of Mt Zion Gospel Music Association. We seek to use gospel music as a positive influence in the church, among young people and throughout our communities.

Worship & Faith

Gospel music that encourages worship, strengthens faith and points people toward God.

Encouragement & Hope

Music that brings encouragement, hope and positive messages to people facing life's challenges.

Youth & Creativity

Creating positive opportunities for young people to develop and express their musical gifts.

Peace & Unity

Using music to promote peace, reconciliation, understanding and unity among people.

Musical Development

Encouraging singers, musicians and emerging gospel artists to grow their skills and creativity.

Positive Opportunities

Supporting constructive activities that connect gospel music with youth and community development.

Music & Community Impact

Our music vision extends beyond performance. We want gospel music to contribute positively to people, families and communities.

Strengthening Faith

Encouraging people through worship, gospel messages and music that points to God.

Developing Young Talent

Helping children and young people discover, develop and use their musical gifts positively.

Promoting Peace

Using music as a positive platform for peace, reconciliation, respect and unity.

Building Community

Connecting music, fellowship and service with the wellbeing and development of communities.

Growing Our Music Ministry

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is developing its music ministry and looking forward to creating more opportunities for gospel artists, musicians, young people and communities.

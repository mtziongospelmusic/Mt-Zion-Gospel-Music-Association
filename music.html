Welcome to Our Music Ministry

Proclaiming the Good News Through Gospel Music

Music is at the heart of Mt Zion Gospel Music Association. We believe gospel music can reach hearts, strengthen faith, encourage hope and bring people together.

Through gospel songs, worship and musical creativity, we seek to provide positive opportunities for people to share their gifts and encourage one another.

We especially value the potential of children and young people. Music can help young people discover their talents, build confidence, express themselves positively and develop a sense of purpose and responsibility.

Our Music Focus

Our music ministry is intended to serve both spiritual and community purposes.

🙏 Worship & Faith

Gospel music that encourages worship, prayer, faith and a deeper relationship with God.

🎶 Encouragement & Hope

Music that encourages people facing difficulties and reminds individuals and families that there is hope for the future.

👧 Youth & Creativity

Encouraging children and young people to discover and develop their musical gifts, creativity and confidence.

🕊️ Peace & Unity

Using gospel music as a positive way to encourage respect, reconciliation, peaceful relationships and unity.

🎤 Musical Development

Supporting the growth of musical abilities and creating opportunities for people to share their talents responsibly.

🌱 Positive Opportunities

Encouraging young people to use music and creativity as constructive pathways that can contribute positively to their families and communities.

Music & Community Impact

We believe music can contribute to positive development beyond the stage.

🌱 Developing Young Talent

Encourage children and young people to discover their abilities and develop their musical gifts from an early age.

🤝 Building Relationships

Use music to bring people together and encourage respect, fellowship and positive relationships.

🕊️ Promoting Peace

Encourage peace, reconciliation and unity through gospel messages, worship and community engagement.

❤️ Encouraging Families

Share messages of faith, hope, love and responsibility that encourage individuals and families.

Growing Our Music Ministry

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to develop gospel music as a meaningful ministry and positive pathway for people of all ages. As our association grows, we hope to create more opportunities for worship, musical development, youth participation, peacebuilding and community engagement.

We want supporters to understand clearly where Mt Zion currently stands.

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is not yet formally registered and is currently working toward registration. The association does not yet have an official organizational bank account. An official bank account is intended to be established after formal registration is completed.
Because registration and banking arrangements are still being established, supporters should contact the association directly before making any contribution so that current support arrangements can be explained clearly.

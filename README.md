# Mt Zion Gospel Music Association

**Proclaiming the Good News Through Gospel Music**

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is a Christian gospel music and community ministry initiative focused on using gospel music, worship, fellowship, and community engagement to proclaim the Good News of Jesus Christ.

## Our Vision

To see gospel music become a powerful instrument for spreading the Good News of Jesus Christ, strengthening faith, inspiring hope, promoting peace and reconciliation, building unity, and developing the gifts and talents of people in our communities.

## Our Mission

To use gospel music, worship, fellowship and community ministry to proclaim the Good News, encourage faith, promote peace and reconciliation, nurture musical talent, strengthen unity, support positive youth development, and contribute to the wellbeing and development of our communities.

## Our Focus

* Gospel music and worship
* Christian fellowship and ministry
* Children and youth development
* Musical talent and creativity
* Peace and reconciliation
* Community unity and wellbeing
* Positive opportunities for young people
* Care, inclusion, and community service

## Core Values

**Faith • Unity • Hope • Service**

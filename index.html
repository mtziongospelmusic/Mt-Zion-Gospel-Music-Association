Welcome to Mt Zion

We seek to use gospel music and ministry to encourage faith, inspire hope, strengthen unity, and serve our communities.

Gospel Music

Promoting gospel music that shares the Good News, encourages faith, inspires hope, and develops musical gifts and talents.

Ministry

Supporting worship, fellowship, youth development, peace, reconciliation, and positive community engagement.

Community

Contributing to peaceful, united and caring communities through service, inclusion, practical support, and positive opportunities.

Vision & Mission

Our purpose is rooted in faith, music, service, and community.

Our Vision

To see gospel music become a powerful instrument for spreading the Good News of Jesus Christ, strengthening faith, inspiring hope, promoting peace and reconciliation, building unity, and developing the gifts and talents of people in our communities.

Our Mission

To use gospel music, worship, fellowship and community ministry to proclaim the Good News, encourage faith, promote peace and reconciliation, nurture musical talent, strengthen unity, support positive youth development, and contribute to the wellbeing and development of our communities.

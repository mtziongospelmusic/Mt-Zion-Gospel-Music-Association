Welcome to Mt Zion

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association exists to promote and support gospel music and Christian ministry while providing opportunities for gospel musicians, artists and ministries to share their gifts.

Through music, worship, fellowship and community programs, we seek to encourage people and proclaim the Good News of Jesus Christ.

Our desire is to see gospel music used as a powerful means of faith, encouragement, worship and ministry.

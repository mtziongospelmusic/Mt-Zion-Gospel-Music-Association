Our Mission

Our work is centred on faith, music, ministry and service.

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to proclaim the Good News through gospel music while encouraging faith, peace, unity and hope. We also seek to create opportunities for people, especially young people, to develop and use their musical gifts in ways that honour God and contribute positively to the community.

What We Value

These values help guide our music, ministry and community activities.

Faith

Keeping Christ and the Gospel at the heart of our ministry.

Unity

Building fellowship and positive relationships through music.

Hope

Using music and ministry to encourage and inspire others.

Service

Using our gifts and opportunities to serve God and community.

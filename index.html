Welcome to Mt Zion

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is dedicated to using gospel music and Christian ministry to proclaim the Good News, encourage faith, inspire hope, promote unity, and serve our communities.

Gospel Music

Promoting gospel music that encourages faith, worship, hope and spiritual growth.

Ministry

Supporting worship, fellowship and ministry through gospel music and Christian service.

Community

Encouraging unity, youth development, positive values and meaningful community involvement.

Our Vision & Mission

Our vision and mission guide the purpose of Mt Zion Gospel Music Association and shape our commitment to gospel music, ministry, fellowship and service.

🌟

Our Vision

To see gospel music become a powerful instrument for spreading the Good News of Jesus Christ, strengthening faith, inspiring hope, promoting peace and reconciliation, building unity, and developing the gifts and talents of people in our communities.

🙏

Our Mission

To use gospel music, worship, fellowship and community ministry to proclaim the Good News, encourage faith, promote peace and reconciliation, nurture musical talent, strengthen unity, support positive youth development, and contribute to the wellbeing and development of our communities.

What We Value

Our work is guided by Christian values that shape our music, ministry, relationships and service.

Faith

Trusting God and sharing the message of the Gospel through music and ministry.

Unity

Bringing people together through fellowship, worship, music and shared Christian values.

Hope

Encouraging people with messages of hope, peace, love and spiritual strength.

Service

Using our gifts, talents and resources to serve God and contribute positively to our communities.

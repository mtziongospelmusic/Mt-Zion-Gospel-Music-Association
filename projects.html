Our Development Focus

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to develop projects that connect gospel music and Christian values with youth development, peace, community wellbeing and practical support.

Our projects are being developed around the belief that meaningful community development begins with people. We want to encourage children and young people from an early age, strengthen families, promote peaceful relationships and identify practical ways to support communities.

Some initiatives may begin as small community activities and grow over time as partnerships, resources and opportunities become available.

Community Development Priorities

Our development approach is people-centred and focused on practical, positive and sustainable community outcomes.

Children & Young People

Encourage positive development from childhood through adolescence and help young people discover their potential.

Families

Encourage values, responsibility, respect and positive opportunities that strengthen families.

Peaceful Communities

Encourage reconciliation, respect, forgiveness, dialogue and unity among people and communities.

Practical Needs

Identify community priorities such as safe water and other essential development needs.

Inclusion

Seek ways to support marginalized and vulnerable people and encourage their participation in community life.

Positive Opportunities

Encourage constructive pathways through music, creativity, skills, mentorship, service and community participation.

Our Project Approach

We want every project to be purposeful, responsible and focused on the needs of the people it is intended to serve.

1. Identify Needs

Listen to communities and identify genuine needs, opportunities and priorities.

2. Build Partnerships

Seek cooperation with churches, communities, families, organizations and other appropriate partners.

3. Develop Solutions

Work toward practical initiatives that encourage positive, responsible and sustainable development.

Growing the Vision

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is developing a long-term vision where gospel music and community development work together to strengthen faith, encourage young people, promote peace and reconciliation, support families, assist marginalized people and contribute to healthier communities.

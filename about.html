About Mt Zion

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association

Faith • Music • Ministry • Fellowship • Hope

Who We Are

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is dedicated to proclaiming the Good News through gospel music, Christian ministry, fellowship and service.

We believe gospel music can encourage faith, inspire hope, strengthen fellowship, develop musical gifts and bring positive influence to individuals, families and communities.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is centered on faith, gospel music, ministry, fellowship and positive community involvement.

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to provide a platform where gospel music and Christian ministry can work together to communicate messages of faith, hope, love and encouragement.

Through gospel music and ministry, we aim to encourage people to use their gifts and talents positively while creating opportunities for fellowship, worship and service.

We also recognize the importance of young people and the wider community. We therefore seek to encourage creativity, positive values, unity and meaningful participation through music and community-focused activities.

Our Vision & Mission

Our vision and mission provide direction for the work and future development of Mt Zion Gospel Music Association.

🌟

Our Vision

To see gospel music become a powerful instrument for spreading the Good News of Jesus Christ, strengthening faith, inspiring hope, promoting unity, and developing the gifts and talents of people in our communities.

🙏

Our Mission

To use gospel music, worship, fellowship and community ministry to proclaim the Good News, encourage people in faith, nurture musical talent, and contribute positively to our communities.

Our Core Values

These values guide the way we approach gospel music, ministry, fellowship and service.

Faith

Trusting God and sharing the message of the Gospel through music and ministry.

Unity

Encouraging fellowship, cooperation and togetherness through gospel music and Christian values.

Excellence

Developing our gifts and talents while pursuing quality in music, ministry and service.

Service

Using our abilities and resources to serve God and contribute positively to our communities.

Our Areas of Focus

Our work is built around several connected areas that support our vision and mission.

Gospel Music

Promoting gospel music that communicates faith, worship, encouragement and hope.

Worship & Ministry

Encouraging worship, fellowship and Christian ministry through gospel music and service.

Youth & Community

Encouraging young people and communities through positive values, creativity, unity and participation.

Our Commitment

Proclaiming the Good News Through Gospel Music

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is committed to building a meaningful gospel music and ministry platform that encourages faith, develops talent, promotes fellowship and serves communities.

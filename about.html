About Mt Zion Gospel Music Association

Proclaiming the Good News Through Gospel Music

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is a gospel music-focused association committed to using music as a meaningful way to share the Good News, encourage faith, support ministry and strengthen fellowship.

Who We Are

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to provide a platform where gospel musicians, singers, worship ministers and supporters can connect, develop their gifts and contribute positively to gospel music and ministry.

Through music, worship, fellowship and community activities, we seek to create opportunities for people to share their gifts, encourage one another and contribute to a positive Christian environment.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To encourage and support the growth of gospel music and ministry, helping gospel music become a meaningful means of sharing Christian faith, hope and encouragement.

Our Mission

To promote gospel music, encourage musicians and worship ministers, support ministry activities and create opportunities for gospel music to reach individuals, churches and communities.

Our Core Values

Our values guide the way we approach gospel music, ministry, fellowship and service.

Faith

Keeping Christian faith at the heart of our music and ministry.

Unity

Encouraging fellowship and cooperation among gospel musicians and supporters.

Excellence

Encouraging quality, creativity and responsible service through gospel music.

Service

Using music and ministry to encourage, inspire and serve others.

Our Areas of Focus

We seek to develop activities that connect gospel music with ministry, fellowship, youth participation and community.

🎵

Gospel Music

Encouraging gospel music, singing, musicianship and creative expression.

🙏

Worship & Ministry

🌱

Youth & Community

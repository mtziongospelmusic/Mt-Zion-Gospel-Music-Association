Who We Are

Proclaiming the Good News Through Gospel Music

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is a Christian gospel music and community-focused association with a vision to use gospel music, Christian values and community engagement to encourage faith, hope, peace, unity and positive development.

We believe that gospel music can do more than entertain. It can encourage people, strengthen families, inspire young people, promote reconciliation and help communities work together for a better future.

Our developing vision includes supporting children and young people from childhood through adolescence, encouraging them to discover their gifts, respect one another, develop positive values and grow into responsible people who can contribute to their families and communities.

Our Vision & Mission

Building faith, peace, unity and hope through gospel music and community development.

Our Vision

To see gospel music become a powerful instrument for spreading the Good News of Jesus Christ, strengthening faith, inspiring hope, promoting peace and reconciliation, building unity, and developing the gifts and talents of people in our communities.

Our Mission

To use gospel music, worship, fellowship and community ministry to proclaim the Good News, encourage faith, promote peace and reconciliation, nurture musical talent, strengthen unity, support positive youth development, and contribute to the wellbeing and development of our communities.

Our Core Values

Values that guide our vision, ministry and community engagement.

Faith

Following and proclaiming the Good News of Jesus Christ.

Unity

Encouraging respect, cooperation and togetherness.

Peace

Promoting reconciliation, forgiveness and peaceful relationships.

Service

Serving people and contributing positively to our communities.

Our Development & Community Focus

We seek to support people, families and communities through constructive and meaningful development initiatives.

🎵 Gospel Music & Ministry

We seek to use gospel music, worship and Christian ministry to proclaim the Good News, encourage faith and provide opportunities for people to develop and share their musical gifts.

👧 Youth Development & Empowerment

We believe every young person has God-given potential. We aim to support and encourage children and young people from childhood through adolescence by nurturing their talents, character, faith, confidence and sense of responsibility.

We want to encourage young people to respect one another, build positive relationships, develop useful skills and become people who can contribute to the wellbeing and development of their own families and communities.

🕊️ Peace, Reconciliation & Unity

We seek to use gospel music, Christian values and community engagement to encourage peace, reconciliation, forgiveness, respect and unity among children, young people, families and communities.

💧 Community Development

We seek to contribute to the wellbeing and development of communities by supporting practical needs and meaningful initiatives, including areas such as access to safe water, community awareness, family support and other community development priorities.

🤝 Support for Marginalized People

We aim to give attention to people who are marginalized, vulnerable or facing difficult circumstances, seeking ways to encourage dignity, inclusion, hope and positive participation within families and communities.

🌱 Families & Positive Futures

We believe strong families and positive opportunities for young people can contribute to healthier communities. We seek to encourage values, skills, responsibility and constructive pathways that help people contribute positively to their families and communities.

Our Community Commitment

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to grow beyond music by becoming a positive force for faith, youth development, peace, reconciliation, unity and community wellbeing. Our future development will focus on encouraging children and young people, supporting families, promoting peaceful relationships and identifying practical ways to assist communities and people who need support.

Growing Together

Our vision is developing, and we welcome people who share a desire to use gospel music and positive community action to make a difference.

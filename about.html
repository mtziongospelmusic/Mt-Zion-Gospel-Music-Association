About Us

Discover the heart, vision and mission of Mt Zion Gospel Music Association.

Who We Are

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is a gospel music and Christian ministry organization established with a passion for proclaiming the Good News through gospel music.

We seek to bring together gospel artists, musicians, worship leaders, ministries and supporters who share a commitment to using music to encourage people and strengthen Christian faith.

Our work is centered on gospel music, worship, fellowship, ministry programs, community engagement and the development of opportunities for gospel musicians and emerging artists.

We believe that gospel music can reach hearts, encourage lives and provide a meaningful way to communicate the message of hope, faith and salvation.

Our Vision & Mission

The foundation that guides the work of Mt Zion Gospel Music Association.

Our Vision

To see gospel music grow as a powerful expression of Christian faith, worship and ministry, while creating opportunities for gospel musicians and artists to use their gifts for God's glory.

Our Mission

To promote gospel music and Christian ministry, encourage gospel musicians and artists, support meaningful music projects, and proclaim the Good News through music, worship, fellowship and community engagement.

Our Core Values

Principles that guide our activities, relationships and ministry.

✝️

Faith

We seek to keep Christian faith at the center of our music and ministry.

🎵

Excellence

We encourage quality, creativity and dedication in gospel music.

🤝

Unity

We encourage fellowship and cooperation among artists, musicians and ministries.

❤️

Service

We seek to use our gifts and activities to serve God and encourage people.

Our Objectives

Areas in which Mt Zion Gospel Music Association seeks to make a positive contribution.

  • Promote gospel music and Christian worship.
  • Encourage and support gospel artists, musicians and emerging talent.
  • Create opportunities for gospel music programs, concerts and events.
  • Encourage collaboration among gospel musicians, ministries and churches.
  • Support music projects that communicate Christian faith and positive values.
  • Use gospel music as a means of encouragement, fellowship and ministry.
  • Develop programs that contribute to the growth and development of gospel music.
  • Build a strong and responsible gospel music community.

Be Part of the Vision

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association welcomes artists, musicians, ministries, supporters and people who share a passion for gospel music and Christian ministry.

About Mt Zion Gospel Music Association

Proclaiming the Good News Through Gospel Music

Who We Are

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is a gospel music-focused association committed to using music as a means of sharing the Good News, encouraging faith, and supporting gospel music ministry.

We seek to provide a platform where gospel musicians, singers, worship ministers and supporters can connect, develop their gifts and contribute positively to gospel music and ministry.

Our Vision

To encourage and support the growth of gospel music and ministry, helping gospel music become a meaningful means of sharing Christian faith and hope.

Our Mission

To promote gospel music, encourage musicians and worship ministers, support ministry activities, and create opportunities for gospel music to reach individuals, churches and communities.

Our Core Values

Faith

Keeping Christian faith at the heart of our music and ministry.

Unity

Working together and encouraging fellowship among gospel musicians and supporters.

Excellence

Encouraging quality, creativity and responsible service through gospel music.

Service

Using music and ministry to encourage, inspire and serve others.
